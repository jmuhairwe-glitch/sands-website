## Confirmed inclusive package selling prices (supersedes additive component quoting)

- **Two or more tanks: UGX 3,500,000 per tank (2=7m; 3=10.5m; 6=21m).** No extra pump charge for the first six tanks, and the standard package already includes the tank, pump, aerator, filtration (UGX 600,000 per tank), piping/installation, initial fingerlings, and **one bag of starter feeds per tank**.
- For budgeting, assume **589 initial fingerlings per tank** to target 500 harvested at 85% survival. This is an interpretation of "fingerlings included"; SANDS needs to verify the package covers the mortality allowance, not simply 500 supplied.
- Starter bag weight has NOT been provided. Set an **editable 25 kg/tank example**, explicitly labelled unconfirmed, and subtract its assumed weight from the first-cycle purchased feed. **Do not bill it twice.** Customer should not be shown this as a confirmed included quantity.
- First-season extra feed costs **UGX 2,500/kg**; in later seasons the farmer must purchase new fingerlings and **all** feed. Repeat-cycle fingerling unit price UGX 500 is still unconfirmed.
- Additional backup power **UGX 1,200,000** is provisionally costed separately as NOT INCLUDED because the package inclusions did not explicitly mention backup power. Verify this with SANDS before issuing quotes.
- SANDS has stated 1x 30,000 L/hour, 300 W pump can serve up to six tanks. For more than six tanks, obtain an itemised quote for extra pumping/filtration rather than assume the retail package covers scaling indefinitely.
- The old tank/pump/filter/aerator/piping component numbers are NOT added on top of the confirmed selling package. They may be kept for internal margin analysis only.

Payback now uses cumulative actual cash flows: first cycle incurs inclusive package + separately priced first-season operating inputs; subsequent seasons incur new fingerlings and full feed (and electricity, tests, support, water). Recovery occurs in the first full cycle where accumulated harvest receipts exceed accumulated outlays, excluding loan interest and unpriced costs.

## Water circulation

SANDS uses a shared pump with flow controlled through a manifold. Tank inlets receive the specified share; nominal leftover water is returned through an aerating/final filtration stage. This return is **not** proof of oxygenation or filtration and cannot short-circuit solids removal/biofilter treatment.

Preliminary per-tank flow = 3,142 L x 1.25 turnovers/hour = about **3,928 L/h**; six tanks together = **23,565 L/h**, leaving **6,435 L/h** of nominal 30,000 L/h capacity for the return route. These figures presume the full 30,000 L/h is delivered at actual installed head; verify pump curve, pipe resistance, filter head and actual water delivery.

One pump is priced per six standard tanks, and a second pump is added at the seventh tank. Bigger/nonstandard tank volumes may need an extra pump sooner if the required flow exceeds 30,000 L/h.

## Makeup water and daily exchange

SANDS specifies daily replacement of **at least 20% of actual working water volume** with new source water. The baseline standard tank uses a provisional 1.0 m fill depth and **3,142 L** working volume, giving approximately **628.4 L of NEW water/day** and an equal quantity leaving via controlled wastewater discharge. Six tanks need **3,770.4 L/day**; 180-day exchange totals approximately **678,672 L**. The first new-installation cycle additionally needs initial fill of **3,142 L/tank** (18,852 L for six tanks). Evaporation, splash, solids purge and cleaning may need further source water. The pricing field for source water starts at UGX 0/1,000 L **explicitly meaning UNPRICED**, not free; source pumping, treatment, disposal and water quality tests may increase actual costs. Return to the final filter/aeration stage is recirculated water, and does not count as new water.

## Mortality and output

Per standard tank at 85% assumed survival:
- Target **500 harvested** x **1.0 kg** = **500 kg** sales biomass.
- Stock **ceil(500 / 0.85) = 589** fingerlings **per tank**.
- Working-volume harvest density = **~159 kg/m3** at 3,142 L, an extremely intensive target requiring verified gas transfer, TAN removal, solids handling, backup and feed loading. A pump flow rating alone cannot validate safe stocking.
- Feed estimate = (500 - 589 x 0.025) x 1.3 x 1.08 = **~681 kg** per tank.
- Sales potential = **UGX 7,500,000/tank** at UGX 15,000/kg, *not guaranteed*.


## Updated aeration electricity (SANDS reported 40 W)

With one 300 W shared circulation pump, **one 40 W aerator per tank (provisional grouping)**, 24-hour continuous operation, 180 production days and the **unverified** UGX 850/kWh tariff:

- One tank = (300 + 40) W x 24 h x 180 d = 1,468.8 kWh; **UGX 1,248,480** (previously UGX 1,468,800 with assumed 100 W aeration).
- Six tanks = (300 + 6 x 40) W x 24 h x 180 d = 2,332.8 kWh; **UGX 1,982,880** (previously UGX 3,304,800).

If the 40 W aerator serves multiple tanks, the totals should be reduced accordingly; verify dissolved-oxygen adequacy before doing so. The hydraulic recirculation pump and fresh-water exchange remain separate calculations.
